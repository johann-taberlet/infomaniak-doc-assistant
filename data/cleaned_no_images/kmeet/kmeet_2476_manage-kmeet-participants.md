# Manage k Meet participants

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2476/manage-kmeet-participants

---

This guide explains how to manually allow or deny participants to a [k Meet](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2474) meeting.

### Preamble

- To approve participants, you must have the status of **moderator**.
- To be a **moderator**, you must either be **the first to connect** or **be designated later**.
- Generally, a person generates a k Meet and will therefore be the moderator throughout the meeting.
- However, if a meeting is created from the [Infomaniak calendar](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/956), a k Meet link can be added, which will be accessible to all event participants; it is therefore recommended that the event initiator **connect a little** **before** the participants to configure their meeting (add a waiting room or a [password](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2464) etc.).

## Approve or deny participants

To be a moderator:

1. Start **k Meet** via [kmeet.infomaniak.com](https://kmeet.infomaniak.com) or from one of the [apps](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/apps.kmeet) for computers or mobile devices.
2. Click the button to start a new meeting:

3. Click the chevron below the meeting name to expand the advanced options.
4. Activate **participant control:**

5. **Start** the meeting.
6. Share the **meeting link or code** and, if necessary, the **password** and/or **encryption key** with your participants.
7. When your participants try to join the meeting, an audio notification invites you to allow or deny them to join the room:

8. You can also set a participant as a **new moderator** during the meeting, or just below, **exclude** them from the meeting:

9. The moderator can also remove the "waiting room" enabled in point 4 above once the meeting has started: Click on the icon ○○○ in the toolbar. Click on Security options: Disable the waiting room to no longer have to manage new arrivals in the meeting:
