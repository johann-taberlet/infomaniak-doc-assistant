# Join a k Meet meeting

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2473/join-a-kmeet-meeting

---

This guide explains how to join a video conference [k Meet](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/meet), the free video conferencing solution by Infomaniak.

### Preamble

- To quickly obtain and share the link to the current meeting, the creator must click on the user icon with a plus sign (`+`):

## Connect to a meeting room

To join a k Meet that an administrator has already started:

1. Start k Meet via [kmeet.infomaniak.com](https://kmeet.infomaniak.com) or from one of the [applications](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/apps.kmeet) for computer or mobile devices.
2. Click the button to **Join a Meeting**.
3. Enter the **code** for the meeting (e.g., `001-0002-003`) or its **link** (which you can also open directly) - read the preamble below if you do not know how to obtain the discussion link.
4. Click the **OK** button:

5. If necessary, authorize access to your [webcam](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2452) and [microphone](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2441) from your **browser** or from one of the applications:

6. Enter a **pseudonym** or **your first and last name.**
7. Adjust the settings according to your preferences (e.g., if you want to disable the camera).
8. Click the blue button to join the meeting:

9. If necessary, enter the [access password and/or encryption key](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2464) provided by the meeting organizer.
