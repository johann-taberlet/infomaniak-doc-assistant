# Rename a k Drive

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2274/rename-a-kdrive

---

This guide explains how to modify the product information of [k Drive](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/ksuite.kdrive). Note that there may be a delay for the applications (desktop, mobile...) to be synchronized with the new information.

## Rename a k Drive...

### ... from the Infomaniak Manager

1. [Click here](https://manager.infomaniak.com/v3/ng/products/ksuite/kdrive) to access the management of your product on the Infomaniak Manager ([need help?](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1990#nav)).
2. Click on the action menu **⋮** located to the right of the item concerned.
3. Click on **Rename the product**.

4. Rename your k Drive.
5. Click on the **Save** button.

### ... from the unified app settings

1. [Click here](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive) to access the Infomaniak **k Drive** Web app (online service [ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive)).
2. Click on the **Settings** icon at the top right of the interface.
3. Check or select the k Drive concerned in the dropdown menu of the left sidebar.
4. Click on **General**.
5. Click on the **pencil**✎ icon to edit the name of the k Drive as you wish:
