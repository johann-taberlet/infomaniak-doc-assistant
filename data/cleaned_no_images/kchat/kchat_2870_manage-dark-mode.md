# Manage dark mode

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2870/manage-dark-mode

---

Infomaniak tools, such as the Infomaniak **Mail** web app (online service [ksuite.infomaniak.com/mail](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/mail)) or the Calendar, Contacts, k Suite interfaces... can be displayed in **light** mode or **dark** mode ("dark mode"), including based on the settings of your current **operating system**.

## Enable manual mode

To manually choose a dark or light display, **without relying on a possible different display on the operating system side**:

1. [Click here](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/all?global-settings=user-account) or on the badge with your initials / avatar in the top right of the Infomaniak Manager) to "**Manage my account**":

2. Then click on **Appearance** in the left sidebar.
3. Click on **Light** to apply the light mode:

4. Click on **Dark** to enable the "dark mode".

## Enable automatic mode

The automatic mode is based on the parameters of your operating system (macOS / Windows...):

1. [Click here](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/all?global-settings=user-account) or on the badge with your initials / avatar in the top right of the Infomaniak Manager) to "**Manage my account**":
2. Click on **Appearance**in the left sidebar.
3. Click on the **Automatic** mode:

Then if you want to modify the **light** or **dark** theme of your computer, so that the Manager's **automatic** mode takes into account your preferences...

### ... on macOS

1. Click on **System Preferences**...

2. Click on **Appearance**and choose the dark mode on the right:

### ... on Windows

1. Open the start menu
2. Click on **Settings**:

3. Click on **Personalize** then **Colors**:

4. Click on **Dark**:

## And on mobile?

The dark theme is enabled on mobile devices for Infomaniak login and account creation pages; it automatically adapts to the mobile system preferences, thus providing a consistent visual experience between mobile applications and login / account creation.
