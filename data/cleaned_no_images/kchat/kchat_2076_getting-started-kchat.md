# Getting started: k Chat

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2076/getting-started-kchat

---

Thank you for choosing Infomaniak and [k Chat](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/kchat) as your discussion solution between you, your circle, or your company! These guides will help you organize your channels, users, conversations, and more!

### Preamble

- Multiplatform, [k Chat](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/kchat) is the instant messaging of my k Suite that allows you to exchange, share, and coordinate your teams securely from…… your web browser… your iOS / Android device… your macOS / Windows / Linux computer
- [Downloadthe k Chat app](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/144) on your devices!It is not possible to modify the location of the kchat-desktop folder on your hard drive. The content of conversations, photos, videos, is stored online on Infomaniak's secure servers.
