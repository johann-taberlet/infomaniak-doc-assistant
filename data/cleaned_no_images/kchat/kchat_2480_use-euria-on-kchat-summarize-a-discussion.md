# Use Euria on k Chat (summarize a discussion)

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2480/use-euria-on-kchat-summarize-a-discussion

---

This guide allows you to quickly discover the essential functions of **Euria**, the artificial intelligence at your disposal within **k Suite**, particularly powerful for summarizing discussions on the [k Chat](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/kchat) app.

### Preamble

- Euria can help you perform all kinds of calculations, translations, provide information on various subjects, and answer your questions.
- Be aware of [this other guide](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1369) regarding the model used and the responsibilities of each party.
- As soon as a new user is added, they receive a welcome message from **Euria**.
- Be aware of [this other guide](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2840) regarding the possibilities of discussing with the AI in k Chat.

## Automatically summarize a k Chat message
