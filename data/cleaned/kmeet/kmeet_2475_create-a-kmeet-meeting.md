# Create a k Meet meeting

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2475/create-a-kmeet-meeting

---

This guide explains how to start an online meeting with [k Meet](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/meet), Infomaniak's ethical and free video conferencing solution.

### Preamble

- There is no limit to the number of participants with paid k Suite offers.
- Participants can [write](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2478) in a shared chat, [annotate](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2620) a [screen share](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2477) and even [control a remote device](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2622).
- To create a meeting room **in advance** and invite participants by creating an associated event in the Infomaniak calendar, refer to [this other guide](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/956).
- It is also possible to share the video stream within a [Video Streaming Broadcast](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1355) (for example, to avoid depending on the connection to k Meet).

## Create a virtual meeting room

To do this:

1. Start k Meet via [kmeet.infomaniak.com](https://kmeet.infomaniak.com) or from one of the [applications](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/apps.kmeet) for computers or mobile devices.
2. Start a new meeting:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/33df8a1369a26d4bb1b03dcdbf1040e8b4caf0c9.png)

3. Enter a **name** for your meeting room.
4. Allow access to your webcam and microphone from your browser or from the application ([click here](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2441) if you encounter any issues).
5. Enter a **pseudonym** or your **first and last name**.
6. If necessary, enable [participant control](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2476), a [password](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2464) or a [custom encryption key](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2464).
7. Click on **Join the meeting**.
8. **Share the room link** to invite participants to join you:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/334fdcc6d02c740cf14deddacb6768390c68a7b2.png)
