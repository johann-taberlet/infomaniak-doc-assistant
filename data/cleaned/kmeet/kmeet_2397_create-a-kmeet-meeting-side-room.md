# Create a k Meet meeting side room

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2397/create-a-kmeet-meeting-side-room

---

This guide covers the "Breakout Rooms" or “side rooms” that can be configured during an online meeting organized with [k Meet](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/meet) the ethical free video conferencing solution by Infomaniak.

### Preamble

- These subgroup rooms allow you to **divide a main meeting into several simultaneous discussions** in distinct virtual spaces.
- Only a [moderator](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2476) can create these rooms and manage participants between the main room and the subgroups.

## Create a side room

To add one or more side rooms:

1. Start k Meet as a moderator via [kmeet.infomaniak.com](https://kmeet.infomaniak.com) or from one of the [applications](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/apps.kmeet) for computer or mobile devices.
2. Once the participants are in the meeting, click on the multi-user iconin the k Meet toolbar.
3. Click the button to add a side room:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/ba0a87e7a5dc3bcb659cdf1f55b1234e84c999df.png)

4. Participants can then join the created room or leave it (by displaying the menu with the same button as in point 2 above):

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/762402d90e1f869ad86bc60ee208683ccc32a44f.png)

5. The moderator can also force one of the participants to join one of the side rooms by clicking on the action menu on the right in the list:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/fe1747d86fe96ba54e3f46fd42315d2b15294e2a.png)
