# Draw on k Meet

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2620/draw-on-kmeet

---

This guide explains how to use the **drawing** feature with [k Meet](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/meet), the ethical free video conferencing solution by Infomaniak, which allows for visual collaboration among participants.

## Display a collaborative whiteboard

It is possible to display a white surface allowing all participants to write information:

1. Start the k Meet desktop app, then [create](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2475) or [join](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2473) a meeting room where users must be present.
2. Once in the meeting, click on the action menu **○○○** in the k Meet toolbar.
3. Click on **Display the whiteboard**:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/d3e2d73508a734cbbc0601ddceccaeec058b597d.png)

4. The drawing surface will be displayed to all participants, and each can write what they want.
5. Each participant's cursor is displayed:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/3c1bc1e9b964eb4f634d41bb631fe6b2118f62de.png)

## Draw on a screen share

### Prerequisites

- Have the desktop app [k Meet](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/apps.kmeet) (desktop application on macOS / Windows / Linux).
- Share your entire screen and not just an application window (point 2 below).

Then:

1. Start the k Meet desktop app, then [create](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2475) or [join](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2473) a meeting room where users must be present.
2. One of the participants initiates a [screen share](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2477) (**desktop app only** - also read the prerequisites above).
3. It is then possible for the other meeting participants to collaboratively draw on the screen share: Click on the pencil icon in the bottom toolbar to draw on the screen. Modify the stylus color if necessary: If the screen share is not started from the k Meet application (computer version), this message will be displayed to participants who want to enable the drawing feature:

It is also possible to [take control of the device](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2622) during a screen share.
