# Resolve a video issue on k Meet

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2452/resolve-a-video-issue-on-kmeet

---

This guide helps you resolve any potential camera access issues with [k Meet](https://kmeet.infomaniak.com/), the ethical video conferencing solution offered by Infomaniak.

With current security standards, a **web browser** or an **application** installed on an operating system like Windows or macOS will necessarily ask for your permission [to access the microphone](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2441) and the camera as soon as a video conference begins.

## Authorize camera access

To do this:

1. Start k Meet via [kmeet.infomaniak.com](https://kmeet.infomaniak.com) or from one of the [applications](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/apps.kmeet) for computers or mobile devices.
2. Start a new meeting:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/33df8a1369a26d4bb1b03dcdbf1040e8b4caf0c9.png)

3. Specify a **name** for your meeting room.
4. Continue, a popup should ask you to authorize the browser to access your microphone + camera:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/93bdd63d036a14b3044469c53ba8b3b1fb380cc2.png)

5. If the popup does not appear automatically, check the icons present in the header of your browser:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/5fd8848dce5029a976cb9aa149cec67a560da882.png)

or here:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/b881672414dee27d908a015a950cd413edb6e57d.png)

6. Your browser settings (here: Chrome) may also allow you to adjust the authorization settings and camera selection if there are multiple cameras available:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/c001eea0729345264f5cfbff07ea1cea1e29fae3.png)

### Your camera is not working? Check the physical cover

If your image remains black while the camera is activated, it is possible that the lens is covered by a built-in privacy cover.

Many laptops have a small mechanical slider above the screen:

- Observe the camera lens at the top of your screen.
- If you see a **red** dot or an opaque sticker on the lens, the cover is closed.
- Slide the slider sideways to unblock the image.

> **Note:** Note: Since this device is mechanical, no software setting will be able to activate the image as long as the cover is not manually pushed.
