# Manage multiple k Drive on the mobile app

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2396/manage-multiple-kdrive-on-the-mobile-app

---

This guide explains how to connect and manage multiple [k Drive](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/kdrive) on the mobile app **k Drive** (application for iOS / Android smartphone or tablet). This feature allows you, for example, to access files from a **personal** k Drive and a **professional** k Drive.

### Prerequisites

- Have a [k Drive](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/ksuite.kdrive) or [k Suite](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/ksuite) offer.
- [Install](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/all?global-settings=user-apps) the mobile app **k Drive** (application for iOS / Android smartphone or tablet).

## Access different k Drives from the mobile app

A user can access multiple k Drives if they already work with multiple k Drives outside the app, if they have been added to other k Drives, etc. They will all be accessible as soon as the user is logged in **with the login ID corresponding to these k Drives**.

This is the case on the **Web** app…

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/b654ecca6032c68dedc2b9d70e777b2809985be1.png)
… as on the **mobile** app:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/276d06ee7307dd830b6a2414269651a4808177a9.png)

Once you are logged into your Infomaniak account on the mobile app **k Drive**, the easiest way to access the different k Drives for which your Infomaniak account has access:

1. Click on the **home** icon to be on the home page,
2. Click on the chevron  to the right of the current k Drive name:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/d3936159d11fd290e9a8843a2fd2bf9716a0f8c8.png)

3. On the sliding lower panel, click on the name of the desired k Drive:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/8d4e2213d3ed8d55f90a537a5965e26b5868e9b7.png)

4. You will be switched to the data of the selected k Drive.

## Access and manage other k Drive accounts

To log in with a different Infomaniak account and therefore access other k Drives:

1. Open the **k Drive** app.
2. Press the badge with your initials or avatar at the bottom right.
3. Press the icon to the right of the k Drive name (just to check the k Drives you already have access to).
4. Press **Switch user**:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/88a0327297901d19592350a579533f89322abb54.png)

5. Press **Add a user**:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/495a1cc99287106b2c812642b28a1f8b3cb58cdf.png)

6. Enter the credentials of your Infomaniak account — refer to [this other guide](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1980) if needed.
7. Choose the k Drive to add.
8. It is now possible to switch from one k Drive to another from the menu **User** > **Switch user**, the credentials of the different accounts are preserved.
