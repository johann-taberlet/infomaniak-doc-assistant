- Only the specific conditions **Backup and Storage** that you can find in your Manager are binding.

## View k Drive storage space

To access k Drive:

1. [Click here](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive) to access the Infomaniak **k Drive** web app (online service [ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive)).
2. Click on the chevron  to expand the **k Drive Management** section in the left sidebar.
3. Click on **Storage space** in the left sidebar:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/533606c938e6e10cca64115731ce2cec110258f0.png)

4. Select large files (or [file versions](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2384) that are also counted in the storage space) using the dropdown menu:
![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/57a24219a53e062b296e84c0faba57a22aedf5fd.png)

5. Click on the action menu**⋮** located above your selection to perform an action (deletion, for example):

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/629b921fa20fea394d964e8105114246f9b6e497.png)

## Increase k Drive storage

### Prerequisites

- Have sufficient permissions within the Organization.

To modify k Drive storage:

1. [Click here](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive) to access the Infomaniak **k Drive** web app (online service [ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive)).
2. Click on **Dashboard** in the left sidebar under **k Drive Management**.
3. Click on **Manage**.
4. Click on **Change Offer**:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/c50240935ab29f19e32f0a7a42890528e0c51059.png)
You will be offered the choice to change the offer or to modify the maximum storage space of your k Drive. Once the offer is modified, click on Next and proceed to payment.
