# Manage a user's rights within an Organization

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1633/manage-a-users-rights-within-an-organization

---

This guide explains how to modify the role and permissions **of a user** so that the latter has only a restricted legal role or access to only certain features of your Organization present in the [Infomaniak Manager](https://manager.infomaniak.com).

### Preamble

- Since you opened your account with Infomaniak, you are part of at least one **Organization.**If you were alone and it was not an invitation, an Organization was created in your name and you automatically become the legal representative. Otherwise, you belong to the Organization that invited you (to join its k Suite, to manage a product, etc.).
- You can define if a user…… is part of your Organization and if he must have the role of: Legal representative Administrator Collaborator… is external to your Organization (limited rights): External users are not included in the sharing of calendars and address books for the entire Organization.

## Modify the rights/permissions of a user

### Prerequisites

- Be [Administratoror Legal representative](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1633) within the Organization.
- Note, it is only possible [to assign the role of legal representative](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/284) if you yourself have this role in the Organization.
- Be aware of the different permissions ([access rights to products](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1610) for example) and [administrative contact preferences](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1440) of a user within an Organization.

To modify the role of a user:

1. [Click here](https://manager.infomaniak.com/v3/ng/accounts/rights/users/list) to access user management on the Infomaniak Manager ([need help?](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1990#nav)).
2. Click on the action menu **⋮** located to the right of the user concerned.
3. Click on **Modify the role**:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/cf6bf2336c4708e07ec8dce46fef7706ed1ca2bc.png)

4. Modify the desired role:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/b4f41ba733ba5d47f19e9cbcf1d55b6b46a5a89b.png)

5. Click on the **Save** button.

> **Note:** A summary email is sent to the user whose role is modified.
