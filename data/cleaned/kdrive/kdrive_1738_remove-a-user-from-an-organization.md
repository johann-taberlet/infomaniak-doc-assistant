# Remove a user from an Organization

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1738/remove-a-user-from-an-organization

---

This guide explains how to remove one of the users from your Organization on the [Manager](https://manager.infomaniak.com) Infomaniak.

### Prerequisites

- Be [Administratoror Legal Representative](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1633) within the Organization.
- Know the login password; it will be requested during the process.

> **Note:** Warning, it is impossible to remove a legal representative [if there is no other legal representative](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/284).

## Remove a user from your Organization

To remove one of the users from the Organization:

1. [Click here](https://manager.infomaniak.com/v3/ng/accounts/rights/users/list) to access the management of the Organization's users on the Infomaniak Manager ([need help?](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1990#nav)).
2. Click on the action menu **⋮** to the right of the user concerned.
3. Click on **Remove from the Organization**:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/3550862c303548ae6b090aa47a621212cf431e6a.png)

4. Click on the blue button **Continue**:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/c057aa066fbb8455bba051d748c362a9ecac9497.png)

5. Enter your login password for the Manager.
6. Check the confirmation box.
7. Click on the red button **Remove this user**

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/8f6a906ed91e3328a7412b8e6eca703351bccc01.png)

If the user had access to **k Drive** or was invited to **view a third-party email address**, several intermediate steps will ask you to perform actions with their data and inform you, if necessary, of what happens to the calendars and address books:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/26b22cc63f3564a0bb5a0a9ebfc1f5ca54a15552.png)
