# Manage multiple k Drive (macOS / Windows)

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2458/manage-multiple-kdrive-macos-windows

---

This guide explains how to connect and manage multiple [k Drive](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/kdrive) on a computer (macOS / Windows). This feature allows you, for example, to access the files of a **personal** k Drive and a **professional** k Drive.

### Prerequisites

- Have a [k Drive](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/ksuite.kdrive) or [k Suite](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/ksuite) offer.
- [Have the k Drive desktop app](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2374) (desktop application on macOS / Windows / Linux).

## Accessing different k Drives from the computer

A user connected to the k Drive application on a computer may already have access to multiple k Drives if they are already working with multiple k Drives outside the app, if they have been added to other k Drives, etc. They are all accessible as long as the user is logged in with the identifier corresponding to these k Drives.

## Accessing and managing other k Drives…

To log in with a different account and thus access other k Drives:

### … from the k Drive desktop app (desktop application on macOS / Windows / Linux)

1. Left-click on the desktop app icon in the notification area of your computer (top right on macOS, bottom right on Windows and a double left-click in the taskbar on Linux).
2. Click on the chevron  to the right of the k Drive name (the k Drives you already have access to with your user are displayed).
3. Click on **Add a k Drive**:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/7b3bf886e03bbcdb3f2de4b204c29b07c48ecbe5.png)

4. Click on the badge with your initials or your avatar in the top right corner.
5. Click on **Add an account**:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/317466c44412f49e2a0696938b7220e5b3a340aa.png)

6. Enter the credentials of your Infomaniak account — refer to [this other guide](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1980) if necessary.
7. Choose and configure the k Drive to add (including the directory that should be used on the computer).
8. It is now possible to switch from one k Drive to another when you click on the k Drive icon and then on the chevron  in the window that opens:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/c107880d607f0dc489b2ed0421cf6390b5441697.png)

### … from the k Drive Web app (online service ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive)

1. [Click here](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive) to access the Infomaniak **k Drive** Web app (online service [ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive)).
2. Click on the chevron  to expand the list in the top left of the k Drive interface
3. Click on the k Drive you want to use

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/91411714ecb6e9a65611cfe68a3b5045cea7dbe4.png)

## Disconnect a k Drive from the desktop app

To access the k Drive application settings on your device:

1. Left-click on the desktop app icon in the notification area of your computer (top right on macOS, bottom right on Windows and a double left-click in the taskbar on Linux).
2. Click on the **⋮** icon on the right in the application.
3. Click on **k Drive settings**:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/6b5ca1b7618345a9318d39e2bb94df5ed283fab6.png)

4. Click on the **⋮** icon to the right of the synchronized k Drive, under *Folders*.
5. Choose **Remove synchronization** (only from your device, with no impact on k Drive):

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/5b787092013d0a021a6ba62d2e1d1e3f5315b2c0.png)
