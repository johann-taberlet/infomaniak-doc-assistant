# Save a k Meet meeting on k Drive

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2472/save-a-kmeet-meeting-on-kdrive

---

This guide explains how to start recording an online meeting organized with [k Meet](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/meet), the ethical free video conferencing solution by Infomaniak, to obtain a video of the presentation in .mp4 format stored on [k Drive](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/kdrive) Infomaniak.

### Preamble

- Video recordingis done on the server side,does not generate any load for the participant's computer that launched it,is in .mp4 video format,has a maximum duration of 3 hours.

## Record a k Meet meeting on k Drive

### Prerequisites

- Have a **k Drive** (standalone/independent or as part of a [k Suite](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/ksuite), [my k Suite](http://infomaniak.com/gtl/ksuite.myksuite), etc.).
- Be the moderator of the video conference to be recorded.
- Do not create an [encrypted video conference with a custom key](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2464).

To record the meeting on k Drive:

1. Start **k Meet** via [kmeet.infomaniak.com](https://kmeet.infomaniak.com) or from one of the [apps](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/apps.kmeet) for computers and mobile devices.
2. Once in the meeting, click on the action menu **○○○** in the k Meet toolbar.
3. Click on the icon ◉ **Start recording**:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/66f2cea9431f7e05711046b978120cd4b091840e.png)

4. Confirm the operation and if necessary, log in to your Infomaniak account, then select a k Drive to record the video conference on; then click the button to start:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/5ddbe8afd6900ca9d783df9cc5d2ff94edcdb1b4.png)

5. To stop saving, click on the toolbar again, then choose **Stop recording**:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/50e0080dd57ab23f7c813c1c95b6e47f95cf523e.png)

6. Confirm, then choose whether you want to go to the recording location on k Drive.
7. The recording is available at the root of the chosen k Drive as soon as the virtual room in which the meeting was held is closed (i.e., as soon as all participants have left the video conference).
