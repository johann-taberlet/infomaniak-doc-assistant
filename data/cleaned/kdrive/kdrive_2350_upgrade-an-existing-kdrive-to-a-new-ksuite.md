# Upgrade an existing k Drive to a new k Suite

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2350/upgrade-an-existing-kdrive-to-a-new-ksuite

---

This guide helps you get started with [k Suite](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/ksuite) by integrating an existing **k Drive** in your Organization.

### Preamble

- The k Drive will be an integral part of the new k Suite.
- The **minimum number of users** in the chosen k Suite offer will be based on the number of users in the existing k Drive offer.
- k Drive users will become k Suite users.
- To help you identify the k Suite offer best suited to your needs, refer to [this other guide](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2497).

## Upgrade my k Drive to k Suite

### Prerequisites

- **Not yet have a k Suite in the Organization.**
- Have a paid [k Drive](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/ksuite.kdrive) offer and be out of the trial period.
- Be the Legal Representative or Administrator of the Organization.

Create a new k Suite from your existing k Drive:

1. [Click here](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive) to access the Infomaniak k Drive Web app (online service [ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive)).
2. Click on **Manage**.
3. Click on **Upgrade the offer**.

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/b51ba79b83de6b0a1dd3e994f49908f360146a9f.png)

You can also do this from an information banner visible on the homepage of your [Manager](https://manager.infomaniak.com/) interface:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/ff5bbe381464346a6957c78620da9b1e74513699.png)

> **Note:** You can also integrate your [Mail Service](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2495) if it is currently outside of k Suite.
