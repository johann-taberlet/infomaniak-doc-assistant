# Manage voice messages with k Chat

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2876/manage-voice-messages-with-kchat

---

This guide details the use of voice messages within [k Chat conversations](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1758) from Infomaniak.

## Send a voice message (and automatic transcription)

To record a voice message using the microphone of your device and allow it to be broadcast in the ongoing discussion (private message for example but also all places where you can send a text message):

1. [Click here](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kchat) to access the **k Chat Web app** (online service [ksuite.infomaniak.com/kchat](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kchat)) or open the **k Chat mobile app** (application for iOS / Android smartphone or tablet) or even the **k Chat desktop app** (desktop application on macOS / Windows / Linux).
2. Click on the microphone icon from the new message composition window:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/33545f28da87372c5b74355c74fc40e7c2de46d8.png)

3. Permissions may be requested so that k Chat can access your audio and voice interface:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/c5c21a64c65e718acae7f1f4aa0e81f4205633d8.png)

4. Once the message is being recorded, click on the cross to the right of the recording to delete it, or on the icon **✓** to keep it:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/47df20015cfd9387aedcda10fd24a5d3e90a7c7d.png)

5. Once the message is saved, it is not sent directly but is added to your message, you can listen to it again or delete it, add text, etc.

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/c5d7136479088fba51614955e2142ce5a770cbf4.png)

6. Once the message is sent, it appears in the ongoing discussion with its text transcribed in clear, all in letters.

## Listen to a voice message on k Chat

To listen to the voice message that has been recorded in the ongoing discussion:

1. Click on the ▶ (Play) button to start listening.
2. Click on it again (⏸ (Pause) button) to stop playback.

## Download the voice message

To save the voice message to the location of your choice:

1. Click on the action menu **⋮**to open a menu:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/9aa063e26cae85e61da017b4700b7b14b51a044f.png)

2. **Start the download**of the voice message to your hard drive.
