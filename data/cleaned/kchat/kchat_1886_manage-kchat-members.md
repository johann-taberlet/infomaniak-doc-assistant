# Manage k Chat members

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1886/manage-kchat-members

---

This guide details the management of members of your [k Chat](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/kchat) chat service.

> **Note:** Refer to [this other guide](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2734) if you wish to manage the channels to which new members will automatically belong.

### Prerequisites

- Have sufficient permissions to administer the product in question.
- [Invite a user](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1455) to join k Chat.

## View members of a channel

To view the users who have access to the selected channel:

1. [Click here](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kchat) to access the **k Chat Web app** (online service [ksuite.infomaniak.com/kchat](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kchat)) or open the **k Chat desktop app** (desktop application on macOS / Windows / Linux).
2. Click on the character icon below the channel title.
3. Participants are displayed in a column on the right side of k Chat:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/02a48ceac4274f38c38d288952ccc8be39588bbc.png)

## Invite or remove a member from a private channel

To manage the users who have access to a private channel:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/b0ff1c9d553e59b29024a152f096c6a8f9d13329.png)

1. Click on the character icon below the channel title.
2. Current participants are displayed in a column on the right side of k Chat.
3. The blue **Add** button allows you to invite a k Chat user or a **complete** [work team](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2585) that does not yet have access to this channel:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/f212508e90c4a04a457e578347a2ba677a47c81d.png)

4. The **Manage** button allows you to remove a member from this channel or designate them as **Administrator** of the channel so that they in turn can manage the members:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/267fdc586301a0e300c78dd02ae5de89f682a845.png)
