# Sign a PDF document on the kDrive web app

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1463/sign-a-pdf-document-on-the-kdrive-web-app

---

This guide explains how to sign a file in **PDF** format on the Web app **kDrive** (online service [ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive)). Refer to [this other guide](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2885) if you are looking for information on other possibilities when editing this type of document.

**⚠ Available with:**

### Preamble

- The Web app **kDrive** natively integrates an editor that allows you to sign a PDF document online.
- Your files are [never analyzed and are exclusively stored in Switzerland](https://news.infomaniak.com/collaboration-en-ligne/).

## Create and apply your signature to a PDF

To access kDrive and manage PDF document signatures:

1. [Click here](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive) to access the Web app **kDrive** (online service [ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive)).
2. Open the relevant PDF document.
3. Click the **Edit** button at the top of the document to access the file editor:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/f90c7dd6ad7203bbea3dfe43cf4ea8bea4de868d.png)

4. Click the **Signature** icon in the toolbar.
5. Click **Add a signature**.
6. Choose the signature creation method:Draw freehand with the mouse.Type text and choose the cursive font (example below).Import an image (scan of your handwritten signature, for example) from your device.
7. Type the desired text for the signature and click the **Aa** button to choose the handwriting style.
8. Click on **Save**:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/b493ad42a9944445a1602b51ab66d529db7d266b.png)

9. Once the signature is created, simply click on the **Signature** icon again to display your created signatures.
10. Click on the signature to insert it into the document.
11. The signature is inserted transparently; simply move and resize it as desired.
12. Once finished, save the changes and choose whether to **create a copy** of the modified document or **overwrite** the existing file:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/2c0c49055d8ee37f0c4958a305a32507dda80056.png)
