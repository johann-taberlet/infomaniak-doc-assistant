# Scan with the kDrive mobile app

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2395/scan-with-the-kdrive-mobile-app

---

This guide explains how to scan or digitize documents (multi-page) with the mobile app [kDrive](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/kdrive) ([application](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/all?global-settings=user-apps) for iOS / Android smartphone or tablet).

### Prerequisites

- Have a [kDrive](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/ksuite.kdrive) or [kSuite](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/ksuite) offer.
- [Install](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/all?global-settings=user-apps) the mobile app **kDrive** (application for iOS / Android smartphone or tablet).

## Scan documents

To quickly digitize paper documents and store them on your kDrive:

1. Launch the mobile app **kDrive** on your iOS or Android device.
2. Press the **icon**‍ at the bottom:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/ef0adfae07f501faeddf5fcdee28ec91f558b3b7.png)

3. Press **Scan a document**:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/a434eabb4c37cec075ebbc2367d9719da20b5626.png)

4. If necessary, authorize the accesses requested by the app:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/b220a29903c844c6ea3205dcde208787c46b820c.png)

5. Document search and detection is performed **automatically** as soon as the image is stable:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/0be51b1b9c98a87ffe2354fd3f0a80f30d4f2a95.png)

6. You can switch to **Manual** mode at the top right and trigger the photo yourself when you are ready:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/5c5f30a7de7c132e065aa7057eb7f016c52a6ad3.png)

7. If necessary, crop the document after manually taking the photo:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/39d72ee6fd90a0c251daf2d495608edb8e09dc30.png)

8. Press **KEEP at the top right.**
9. Press the thumbnail at the bottom left to access advanced editing (rotation, black and white...) or to delete a specific page.
10. Press **Finish** at the top left.
11. Repeat the steps to scan other pages or press **Save**at the bottom right:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/9b71ae3a1897108d79f18189d462c7a6e93a4044.png)

12. If your scan contains multiple pages, it will be grouped into a PDF to be saved in the location of your choice on the kDrive. If there is only one image, you can choose to save it in .JPG format:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/b698f3a13d59771afcb9643576c7403ef85eb3c4.png)

13. The document is sent to the kDrive:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/a9b54cbdba38728fc569066814f0a34c52a78fd9.png)

14. You will find it on all your connected devices:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/9832a06d03872545e3f4ced3fb9328b4a0b3ffe4.png)
