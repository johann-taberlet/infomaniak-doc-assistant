# Access kDrive with my kSuite / my kSuite+

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2489/access-kdrive-with-my-ksuite-my-ksuite

---

This guide details the different ways to access kDrive once the **my kSuite offer** is subscribed, in order to view, add and modify files on kDrive:

## 1. From a web browser

To access the **kDrive** web app (online service [ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive)):

1. [Click here](https://manager.infomaniak.com) to access the Infomaniak Manager.
2. Click on the icon in the top right of the interface.
3. Choose **kDrive.**

## 2. From the kDrive app

To access kDrive from a mobile or desktop app:

1. [Install kDrive](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2374) for your device.
2. Open the application.If it is the desktop app, left-click on the desktop app icon in the notification area of your computer (top right on macOS, bottom right on Windows and a double left click in the taskbar on Linux).Refer to this other guide on this subject.

## 3. Via WebDAV (when changing offers)

**⚠ Available with:**

This multiplies the possibilities of connecting to your data, in particular via [the WebDAV application](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2409) of your choice.
