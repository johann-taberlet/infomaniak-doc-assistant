# Clear the kDrive mobile app cache

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1561/clear-the-kdrive-mobile-app-cache

---

This guide explains how to delete the cache (data cached after various operations, unnecessarily taking up storage space on your device) from the mobile app **kDrive** ([app](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/all?global-settings=user-apps) for iOS / Android smartphone or tablet).

## Delete the kDrive cache...

### ... on iOS (iPhone, iPad)

1. Open the mobile app kDrive (iOS).
2. Tap on the photo/initials at the bottom right.
3. Tap on Settings.
4. Tap on Local Storage Management.
5. Delete the data you want, **cache** for example (or **import** which also allows you to unblock any potentially blocked imports)...

### ... on Android

1. Open the settings of the Android phone.
2. Go to the **Applications** or **Apps & notifications** section for example.
3. Tap on **Show XX apps** if the device does not display them by default.
4. Tap on **kDrive**.
5. Go to the **Storage** or **Storage space and cache** section for example.
6. Tap on **Clear cache**.Be careful if you click on the Clear storage button, you will delete all user data (login credentials for example) in addition to the cache.
