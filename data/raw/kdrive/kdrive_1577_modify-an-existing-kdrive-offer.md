# Modify an existing kDrive offer

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1577/modify-an-existing-kdrive-offer

---

This guide explains how to change your [kDrive](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/kdrive) offer, the collaborative storage solution on a 100% Swiss Made cloud.

### Preamble

- **kDrive** is the name given to the file storage/sharing service offered by Infomaniak in Switzerland.
- kDrive is accessible **for free** or by **subscription** depending on whether you are **alone** using it or if you want **collaborators** to also access it.
- kDrive exists as an independent product, or as a product among others within the **kSuite** & **my kSuite** / **my kSuite+** suite.To help you identify the offer most suited to your needs, refer to this other guide.

> **Note:** If you have been invited to view/collaborate on a file from a kDrive, it is possible that you do not actually own the kDrive product; you only have **access** to the kDrive managed by someone else. You cannot therefore interact with the current offer.

## Determine the current kDrive offer

### From the kDrive Web App

To find out which offer you currently have:

1. [Click here](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive) to access the Infomaniak **kDrive** Web App (online service [ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive)).
2. Click on **Manage** to go to the offer change and **view the current offer**: 

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/2dcbdd77c4a76c820addc94cb81eaf7b3b38b4d0.png)

If no dashboard is accessible at the bottom left, you have limited features (e.g., **my kSuite** offer) that can be unlocked by upgrading to a higher offer: 

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/b8b66c2738e9a88c659d42da5f4ea1f40bd8309e.png)

### From the Infomaniak Manager

You can also find out about the current offer from **the Manager, the dashboard of your products**.

If **no kDrive product** is present in the Manager, the kDrive app is accessible **only among the apps at the top right**, because you have…

- either a **my kSuite** / **my kSuite+** offer: 

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/8789e24e95447cc2ac4ab3dcedf149e0d05ad0dc.png)

- or you have been invited during a document share (you are therefore an external user of kDrive):

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/e499387f8b64e64118ef91fac42c8f48b208c9c4.png)

Otherwise, find your **kDrive** among your **collaborative tools**: 

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/9e5211446eb0972d72e1679544ed356f61e78227.png)

## Change the current kDrive plan

Your kDrive can evolve with you. When your needs expand — whether to welcome new collaborators, integrate a family member, or store a growing volume of data — easily increase its capacity to unlock its full potential.

### Prerequisites

- Have sufficient permissions within the Organization.

To modify your current kDrive offer:

1. [Click here](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive) to access the Infomaniak **kDrive** Web App (online service [ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive)).
2. Click on **Manage** to go to the offer change and **view the current offer**: 

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/2dcbdd77c4a76c820addc94cb81eaf7b3b38b4d0.png)

You can then switch to a higher offer from the Infomaniak Shop:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/062f8cfe9ce271ba3a7c01aeacedef11314840f6.png)

If, for example, by choosing a new offer, you increase the quota of users that can be added to your kDrive, refer to [this other guide](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2364) to invite them to join you.
