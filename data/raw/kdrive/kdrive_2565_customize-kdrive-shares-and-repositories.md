# Customize kDrive shares and repositories

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2565/customize-kdrive-shares-and-repositories

---

This guide explains how to add background images and change the text color of [share links](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2382) and [kDrive drop boxes](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2386) so that it matches the graphic identity of your organization.

### Preamble

- Any customization from the **share link** is applied to the **drop box** (and vice versa).
- The drop box customization will only be visible **when you share access to it via a**[share link](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2382).

## Customize share links & drop boxes

To access kDrive customization:

1. [Click here](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive) to access the Infomaniak kDrive Web app (online service [ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive)).
2. Click on the chevron ‍ to expand the **Shares** section in the left sidebar:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/a315e1ef648fd7fc10743f8c87e99205821dc80e.png)
Click on Public share links:… or click on Drop boxes.Click on Customize:
3. Modify the text color and add an image (min. 100 KB; it must be on kDrive) or a background color.
4. Preview the effects on the 2 display types.
5. Click on **Save**:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/940add6f16ec5d279f25cd66c635c1851c7bb246.png)
