# Export an email to kDrive

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/973/export-an-email-to-kdrive

---

This guide explains how to save an email (as if you were [downloading](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1718)) but directly to your [kDrive](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/kdrive). You will thus be able to save in 1 click a message received on an address of your Infomaniak Mail Service, this in `.eml` format readable directly within your kDrive interface.

## Add an email to kDrive…

### … from the Infomaniak Mail Web App

To export the email message of your choice to your kDrive:

1. [Click here](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/mail) to access the Infomaniak Mail Web App (online service [ksuite.infomaniak.com/mail](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/mail)).
2. Open the email you wish to save.
3. At the top right of the email in question:Click on the kDrive folder icon to Add to kDrive.If the icon is not visible, click on the action menu ⋮ and choose Add to kDrive:
4. Choose the kDrive (from those to which you are attached by your Organization) and the desired location on it to save your file - if you rename it, keep the `.eml` extension to be able to read it directly in the kDrive interface:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/89c252d4b5868f88fddc5de8df9ad5696464e2ba.png)

5. Click on the **Add** button…A message at the bottom of the screen notifies you of the progress of the recording and allows you to go directly to the email save location.The saved email can be viewed in kDrive as if you were reading it in Mail directly:

### … from the Infomaniak Mail mobile app

To do this:

1. Open the email in question on the mobile app [Infomaniak Mail](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1860) (application for iOS / Android smartphone or tablet).
2. Click on the action menu **⋮** located at the top right of the displayed email.
3. Choose **Save to kDrive** and follow the same procedure as above in step 4.
