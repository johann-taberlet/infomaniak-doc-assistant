# Manage a kChat channel

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2732/manage-a-kchat-channel

---

This guide details the use and management of channels in [kChat](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/kchat).

### Preamble

- A kChat channel is a communication space dedicated to a project, a theme, or a team in which members can exchange documents, write in real-time, and organize video conferences.
- Private channels (as well as public and guest channels) are limited in number according to the kSuite offer acquired; refer to the comparison table on [the sales page](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/ksuite.prices) for more information.

## General Channel

Every Organization on kChat automatically has a General Channel in which all users are automatically invited.

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/8f39afb7947a7b73e9f89c0ede86e1484ffc2731.png)

You can use this channel to share information that concerns your entire organization, such as company outings or motivating good news.

You can of course start a new [conversation thread](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1758) within the General Channel.

## Differences between PRIVATE or PUBLIC channel

PUBLIC channels increase transparency. All users can see and join a public channel, which ensures that all people involved in a project, topic, or team have access to the same information.

PRIVATE channels allow you to restrict sensitive content to users of your choice. Only an existing member can invite another user to a private channel. There are also differences in the management of archiving/deletion (read more below).

## Convert a public channel to a private channel

To transform a Public channel into a Private channel:

1. From the kChat web app (online service [ksuite.infomaniak.com/kchat](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kchat)), click on the Public channel in the left sidebar menu.
2. Then click on the chevron on the channel title at the top of the discussion.
3. Choose **Convert**:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/cea866a00d862f58006883cb3193a66f5dfe2fd3.png)

The history and membership of members are preserved; publicly shared files remain accessible to anyone with the link; the change is permanent and cannot be undone.

### And the reverse?

The reverse (changing a private channel to a public channel) is also possible, but only a kChat administrator user can do it; the message history and members who already have access to the channel will be preserved; the channel will become accessible to all users of the other public channels of the Organization except external users who will have to be invited manually.

To perform the conversion from kChat management on the Manager:

1. [Click here](https://manager.infomaniak.com/v3/ng/kchat/) to access the management of your product on the Infomaniak Manager ([need help?](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1990#nav)).
2. Click on the dropdown menu to display private channels.
3. Click on the action menu **⋮** located to the right of the item concerned.
4. Click on **Convert to public channel**:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/71323d2f1720965cbde52510eb7fde17987e4e7f.png)

## Leave a channel

In addition to the main channel named General, you can leave a channel at any time:

1. From the **kChat** web app (online service [ksuite.infomaniak.com/kchat](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kchat)), click on the Public channel concerned in the left sidebar menu.
2. Then click on the chevron on the channel title at the top of the discussion.
3. Click on Leave the channel:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/ec3670559ea6589a1d0e35f25642abb6ee6c02cb.png)

If you leave a private channel, you will no longer find it in the search but only via its URL if you have it or by being reinvited by a member of the channel.

If you leave a public channel, you leave it immediately but can rejoin it whenever you want simply by finding it with its name (2) in the search (1), even if it has been archived:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/9b2e261a3d2deb50cf6a0b8f8bff06ef8d5f9a43.png)

## Archive / delete a channel

In addition to the main channel named General, you can archive a channel at any time, which prevents any new messages and will no longer count it in the quota of your available channels. The operation is possible from the [Manager](https://manager.infomaniak.com/v3/ng/kchat/) or directly on the kChat interface:

1. From the **kChat** web app (online service [ksuite.infomaniak.com/kchat](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kchat)), click on the channel in the left sidebar menu.
2. Then click on the chevron on the channel title at the top of the discussion.
3. Click on **Archive the channel**:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/3046d4840361071ac1c906ae5b6e8589d154c996.png)

If you create a channel and then archive it, you can rejoin it whenever you want simply by finding it with its name (2) in the search (1):

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/eaf6ec3f93d000dc4d2400620dfff2469f1c4be7.png)

You will then be able to unarchive it:

1. From the **kChat** web app (online service [ksuite.infomaniak.com/kchat](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kchat)), click on the channel concerned in the left sidebar menu.
2. Then click on the chevron on the channel title at the top of the discussion.
3. Click on **Unarchive the channel**:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/fdc170cc8d34178d0365461b96a16db5681c1ad2.png)

or close it to no longer see it on your interface:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/9c22cc45264b485424a4e40210b39c16707e45b2.png)

You can also delete a channel and in this case, all associated data will be permanently deleted:

1. [Click here](https://manager.infomaniak.com/v3/ng/kchat/)to access the management of your product on the Infomaniak Manager ([need help?](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1990#nav)).
2. Click on the action menu**⋮** located to the right of the item in question.
3. Click on **Delete**:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/e67c195b3786e9f655acc8b5847cbd0897d986e1.png)

 

## Add to favorites

To add any channel to favorites:

1. From the **kChat** web app (online service [ksuite.infomaniak.com/kchat](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kchat)), click on the star icon located at the top of a channel.
2. A new Favorites menu will appear in the left sidebar grouping all the items you have added to favorites (valid for your user only):

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/0a7a354c3a0c25c2e4e29ab01027b89ce3e3ce3c.png)

## Mute a channel

You can hide the [notifications](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1460) of a channel:

1. From the **kChat** web app (online service [ksuite.infomaniak.com/kchat](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kchat)), click on the action menu ⋮ to the right of the channel in question (in the left sidebar).
2. Choose **Mute**:

![sign](https://faq.storage.infomaniak.com/655b619e1f2ba8.51566367gif)
