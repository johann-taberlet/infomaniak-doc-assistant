# Manage kChat conversations

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1758/manage-kchat-conversations

---

This guide details the principle of [kChat](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/kchat) conversations, organized within [private and public discussion channels](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2732), and direct discussions between users.

## Start a conversation

To write to a user in your Organization, click on their name in the list of members on the left side of your kChat interface:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/8c4ee2a83748350b93bd84f1252feba2d9a2d2c2.png)

You can also click on the **+** to the right of **Personal Messages** to select up to 7 members and thus create a group conversation:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/8c40baf29988db8d490a0972b3553ec58f4c5447.png)

Write and send your message by pressing the send button:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/7f7f519b453247bd6a85eaaf661cc83a2f20eced.png)

If necessary, you can attach files up to 100 MB. You can also use “[reactions](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1190)”.

## Scheduled sending: schedule a message

**⚠ Available with:**

******without custom scheduling*

To schedule a message to be sent at a later date/time, simply click on the chevron to the right of the send button and then choose the time the message should be sent:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/728b9e1122e109abc4882d8a21954facebe05f87.png)

**On the kChat mobile app** (application for iOS/Android smartphone or tablet), press **a few seconds** on the send button, and a menu to schedule the message will appear.

## Mention another user

To contact one of the Organization's members (or an entire [work team](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2585)) via a message, type the at sign `@` to display the people or channels to mention:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/2c53aeba0a9f3c5c9df4be98945641a99cd0f672.gif)

> **Note:** Note, depending on the type of channel concerned, mentioning a user displays a message allowing you to add them to the discussion (or notify them) otherwise they will not see the mention.

## Request a read receipt

For users to be able to acknowledge receipt of your message and thus indicate that they have read the message, activate a read receipt before sending the message on the channel, by clicking on the icon `(!)` then activating the toggle switch:

![sign](https://faq.storage.infomaniak.com/65cf74414d7f60.45831983gif)

Once the message is sent, the result will be as follows:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/614ed68038d7425c713e3065848544b32bf0c389.png)

## Edit / delete a message, or pin it

To **edit** a message (which will then have a mention of edited message), click on the action menu to the right of the message to edit. In the same place, you can also **delete** your message which will then disappear from the discussion for all users:

![image](https://faq.storage.infomaniak.com/658d4dad863552.94527387gif)

An indication regarding the deletion will appear to all other users of the channel at the initial location of the message.

In the same place, you can **pin** a message:

- Pinned messages are visible to all members of the channel, whether it is public or private.
- Pinned messages in a private channel are only accessible to members of that channel.
- You can view the list of all pinned messages via the channel's 'Pinned Messages' option.
- To remove a pinned message, hover over it again and select 'Unpin'.

## Organize conversations in kChat

The type of sorting for the items you manage on the left side of the screen is very important.

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/79837ba011cbb60d67be0ed1f51397c596da3862.png)

Imagine opening kChat after several days of absence, unread conversations will be displayed one below the other. After reading them, you may want to find a piece of information read among them, so a sort by recent activity for example (see below for other possibilities) allows you to find the latest conversations that concerned you.

This sorting option is available for all items: discussion channels, categories, direct messages, etc.

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/e7963f98c5f6fad3d9e7def26ea3312412c4ff30.png)

## Search for a conversation

Searching for items (word, participant, file) is at the top of the window:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/c3e9338c6802e752d654ec99dab1c5830585101f.gif)

Once results are found, they appear on the right side of the window.

## Discussion thread

Regardless of the channel or private chat with another user, you can start a discussion thread from another simply by choosing Reply on the desired message.

A discussion thread will open on your right allowing each user of the channel to contribute their comments in this specific thread without disrupting any potential new topics in the current channel.

These discussion threads are then centralized to be read and reread in the **Discussion Threads** section of the left sidebar:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/afbbad3044f668209d227f1d49aa63f292b1fbe3.gif)
