# Use Euria on kChat (summarize a discussion)

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2480/use-euria-on-kchat-summarize-a-discussion

---

This guide allows you to quickly discover the essential functions of **Euria**, the artificial intelligence at your disposal within **kSuite**, particularly powerful for summarizing discussions on the [kChat](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/kchat) app.

### Preamble

- Euria can help you perform all kinds of calculations, translations, provide information on various subjects, and answer your questions.
- Be aware of [this other guide](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1369) regarding the model used and the responsibilities of each party.
- As soon as a new user is added, they receive a welcome message from **Euria**.
- Be aware of [this other guide](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2840) regarding the possibilities of discussing with the AI in kChat.

## Automatically summarize a kChat message

To get the summary of a kChat message:

1. Hover over the message and select the Euria actions icon in the top right.
2. Click on **Summarize the discussion thread**:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/386903032a9d840036e5eefd182d2108255d9d6f.png)
‍
3. Get the summary, generated by AI and sent by kChat Bot in the right sidebar:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/d13a41d409e84b07bbcda9b8f001bb8fe7b01baa.png)
