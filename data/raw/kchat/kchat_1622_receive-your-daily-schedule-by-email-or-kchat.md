# Receive your daily schedule by email or kChat

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1622/receive-your-daily-schedule-by-email-or-kchat

---

This guide explains how to automatically receive a **summary** of your activities scheduled on [Calendar](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/calendar) Infomaniak in your Mail and/or kChat inbox every morning.

### Preamble

- This feature is **disabled** by default on all calendars.
- This summary is sent to your email address (the one used to **log in to your**[Infomaniak account](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2193)).
- It contains a list of all the day's events with their most important information (possible kMeet link, location, times, etc.).You can even imagine forwarding this daily schedule to your team or assistant so they know the best times to contact you, including creating a rule for automatic forwarding based on the criteria corresponding to the received email.

## Obtain a schedule automatically and daily

### Prerequisites

- Sending a summary on kChat requires having [kChat](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/kchat) within your Organization.

To start the day by consulting a message in your Infomaniak inbox containing the schedule based on your Infomaniak agenda:

1. [Click here](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/calendar?settings=personalization__reports) to log in to the **unified settings** of your Infomaniak Web apps and more specifically to the settings of the **daily reminder of the Calendar app**.
2. Check or select the relevant Organization in the dropdown menu of the left sidebar.If you modify it, return to the settings by clicking on the daily reminder item to access the settings:
3. Click the button to **Add a daily reminder** and configure it:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/da44d85a7c56840eb17058dcd9e60481f7509c0e.png)

To customize your reminder according to your needs:

1. Select the relevant calendar.
2. Choose the reception mode (Mail, kChat, or both).
3. Choose the time you want to receive the reminder (the night before or early the same day).
4. Choose whether or not to include [recurring](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1565) events.
5. Choose the days on which the daily reminder will be sent.
6. Confirm your choices at the bottom of the page:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/7a258214c740429ea08f66b22e280da56b93550b.png)

## Disable the daily reminder

To stop receiving the daily summary by **email** or by **Euria** on kChat:

1. Click on the [link](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/calendar?settings=personalization__reports) located at the bottom of the received notification:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/ed3db7523eb8a6cf05db0f1ca2f01cf8b142909d.png)

2. Click on the reminder to delete:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/23798d3684d2a6302c860a6c22bdaf1c83cedd8a.png)

3. Delete the reminder from the edit page:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/c090de432096237cd38d4d26743b1a1d0c8dfcf9.png)
