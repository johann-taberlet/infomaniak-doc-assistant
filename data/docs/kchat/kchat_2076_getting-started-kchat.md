# Getting started: kChat

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2076/getting-started-kchat

---

Thank you for choosing Infomaniak and kChat as your discussion solution between you, your circle, or your company! These guides will help you organize your channels, users, conversations, and more!

### Preamble

- Multiplatform, kChat is the instant messaging of my kSuite that allows you to exchange, share, and coordinate your teams securely from... your web browser... your iOS / Android device... your macOS / Windows / Linux computer
- [Downloadthe kChat app](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/144) on your devices! It is not possible to modify the location of the kchat-desktop folder on your hard drive. The content of conversations, photos, videos, is stored online on Infomaniak's secure servers.
