# Getting Started Guide: kMeet

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2474/getting-started-guide-kmeet

---

Thank you for choosing [kMeet](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/meet) Infomaniak as a video conferencing solution for individuals & businesses:

- Unlimited audio and video calls
- [Access without registration](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2473)
- Compatibility [Web, mobile and desktop](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/apps.kmeet)
- [Integrated chat](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2478) & reactions (emojis, sounds)
- [Screen sharing](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2477) with [drawing tools](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2620), [remote control](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2622)
- [Breakout rooms](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2397)
- Integration with [calendar](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/956) (video link creation)
- Integration with kDrive for storing [meeting recordings](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2472)
- Integration with kChat for [video calls](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2827)
- [Privacy respect](https://news.infomaniak.com/cloud-ethique/) (no ads, hosted in Switzerland)
- Security with [AES-256 encryption](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2464)
- [Eco-friendly](https://www.infomaniak.com/gtl/ecology) (renewable energy, CO2 compensation)
