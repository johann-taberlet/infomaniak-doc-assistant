# Discuss during a kMeet meeting

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2478/discuss-during-a-kmeet-meeting

---

This guide explains how to use the chat feature through the integrated chat in [kMeet](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/meet) the ethical free video conferencing solution by Infomaniak.

### Preamble

- The chat supports the use of [Emojis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emoji).
- It is also possible to:request the floor via the hand icon (Raise/Lower Hand)react with a visual (and audible) emojicreate a poll among the participants (click on the Polls tab in the chat pane)

## Start the chat between participants

To do this:

1. Start the kMeet app.
2. [Create](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2475) or [join](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2473) a meeting room.
3. Click on the message bubble icon in the toolbar to **Open/Close the chat** and chat from a left sidebar:
