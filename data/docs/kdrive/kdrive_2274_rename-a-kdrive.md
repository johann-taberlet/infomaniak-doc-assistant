# Rename a kDrive

Source: https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/2274/rename-a-kdrive

---

This guide explains how to modify the product information of [kDrive](https://infomaniak.com/gtl/ksuite.kdrive). Note that there may be a delay for the applications (desktop, mobile...) to be synchronized with the new information.

## Rename a kDrive...

### ... from the Infomaniak Manager

1. [Click here](https://manager.infomaniak.com/v3/ng/products/ksuite/kdrive) to access the management of your product on the Infomaniak Manager ([need help?](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/support/faq/1990#nav)).
2. Click on the action menu **⋮** located to the right of the item concerned.
3. Click on **Rename the product**.

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/e94e97fb7887e9ca67f6c459816caa2fffc0cae4.png)

4. Rename your kDrive.
5. Click on the **Save** button.

### ... from the unified app settings

1. [Click here](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive) to access the Infomaniak **kDrive** Web app (online service [ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive](https://ksuite.infomaniak.com/kdrive)).
2. Click on the **Settings** icon at the top right of the interface.
3. Check or select the kDrive concerned in the dropdown menu of the left sidebar.
4. Click on **General**.
5. Click on the **pencil**✎ icon to edit the name of the kDrive as you wish:

![image](https://faq.storage5.infomaniak.com/5debe023357f682e34a112e2f0538d1efd99b044.png)
