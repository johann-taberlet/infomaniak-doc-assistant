# Evaluation Questions

This document lists the 40 questions used to evaluate the RAG system during development.

---

## kMeet (11 questions)

1. How do I create a kMeet meeting?
2. How can I share my screen during a kMeet call?
3. What should I do if my audio is not working in kMeet?
4. How do I create side rooms (breakout rooms) in kMeet?
5. How can I record a kMeet meeting?
6. How do I set a password for my kMeet meeting?
7. How can I livestream a kMeet meeting?
8. How do I enable automatic subtitles or transcription in kMeet?
9. How do I join an existing kMeet meeting?
10. How can I use the whiteboard or drawing feature in kMeet?
11. How can I remotely control another participant's computer in kMeet?
12. How do I fix camera or video issues in kMeet?
